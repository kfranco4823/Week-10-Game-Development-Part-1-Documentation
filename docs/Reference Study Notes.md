## My revised direction

In one short paragraph, explain how the study changed or confirmed your project plan.

Knowing that I would have to make a video game was intimidating right from the start because as someone whose hobby is video games, I set way to high expectations for myself and was fearful
of not being able to meet those expectations. Reading Week 10's webpage as well as watching the video tutorial playlist showed me the step by step and with proper planning is the way to go.
I thought of something fun with my Pom Party Tetris style game and since I play lots of games, I have a good idea as to what can be entertaining and find ways to really keep my players hooked.
Finding the right assets is something that took time and I believe I have good ones now but of course as any project, I am open to changes in the future. The nice thing is that having the general
skeleton of the plan makes modifications so much easier.
